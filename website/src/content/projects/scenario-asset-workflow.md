The public case study is being reconstructed around a clear lifecycle: extraction, normalization, generation, validation, and regression use.

## Questions the case study will answer

- What information must survive the transition from a real event to a simulation asset?
- Which parameters can be generalized without changing the failure mechanism?
- How is a generated asset validated before it enters a regression suite?
- What provenance is required to reproduce an asset later?

The goal is a concise engineering record, not a roadmap or a product pitch.
